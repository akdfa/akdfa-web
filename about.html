About AKDFA / Who We Are

United by passion. Driven by purpose.

Founded with a passion for celebrating sporting excellence, our association proudly holds the distinction of being the world's first registered fans association dedicated to a sports player. Built on the values of unity, loyalty, and sportsmanship, we bring together thousands of fans who share a common admiration for one of the greatest icons in sports.

Over the years, our association has grown into a vibrant community that goes beyond fandom, engaging in social initiatives, charitable activities, sports promotion programs, and community development efforts. Through our collective spirit, we aim to inspire the next generation while honoring the legacy of the player who unites us all.

More than just a fan club, we are a family connected by passion, respect, and a commitment to making a positive impact on society. Our mission reaches beyond the boundary too - supporting local cricketing talent in every district, so young players get the grounds, gear, and encouragement they need to be seen.

Our Journey

From a shared passion to a statewide family

What began as a small circle of fans has grown, unit by unit, into a structured, registered association present across Kerala and beyond.

  1. 01

    A shared passion

    A small group of fans, united by admiration for MS Dhoni, began organizing informally around watch parties, local matches, and community drives.

  2. 02

    Units take shape

    Momentum spread district by district as fans formed local units, each with its own leadership, to organize activities closer to home.

  3. 03

    Official registration

    AKDFA became a registered association (Reg No: 949/17) - the world's first registered fan association dedicated to a sportsperson.

  4. 04

    17 units, one family

    Today, AKDFA connects 17 units across 14 Kerala districts plus Chennai, Bangalore, and a dedicated Ladies unit - united in cricket and community service.
